# Release under the GNU GPL v3

CB21 is free and open source, and anyone may use, change and share it. We chose GPL-3.0 over a permissive license such as MIT so that modified versions of the app have to stay open source too. Relicensing later would need every contributor's agreement.

## Consequences

- Anyone who distributes CB21 or a modified version must provide the source under the same license.
- The GPL conflicts with the Mac App Store's terms, which fits the earlier decision to distribute directly (ADR-0002) rather than through the store.
